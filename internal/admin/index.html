Peers

    
Peering is bidirectional: you configure each remote on our server (below), and send them INN snippets for their server when they agree to carry your feed. See Eternal September peering for the three-file model.

    

    

      Give to an INN peer (our side)
      
Send these three snippets to the remote admin. They paste them into incoming.conf, innfeed.conf, and newsfeeds on their INN box, then add you as a peer the same way you would any other site.

TOP1000 path statistics

    
Peers such as Eternal September expect participation in the TOP1000 project. Path headers from POST and IHAVE are aggregated into ninpaths-compatible reports mailed to top1000@anthologeek.net. Enable with inpaths.enabled: true in config.yml.

    

    

      
      
      
      
    

    
    
Cron example: 6 6 * * * openusenet inpaths flush --config /etc/openusenet/config.yml
10 6 * * * openusenet inpaths report --config /etc/openusenet/config.yml | mail -s "inpaths $(hostname)" top1000@anthologeek.net

Binary flood alerts 

    
Groups flooded with binary-looking traffic get a 7-day retention quota. Review to block, whitelist, or dismiss. Text groups stay forever by default. Authenticated users are limited to 25 binary POSTs/day.
